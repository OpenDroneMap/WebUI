# OpenDroneMap WebUI Trademark Guidelines

OpenDroneMap WebUI is an open source project and [UAV4GEO](https://uav4geo.com) is the steward of its trademark. Because the code is available to download and modify, proper use of the OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark is essential to inform users whether or not UAV4GEO stands behind a product or service. When using OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks you must comply with these guidelines.

The trademark names include:

 * OpenDroneMap WebUI
 * WebODM Lightning

The trademark logos include:

 ![OpenDroneMap WebUI](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f209a5f6-a2d5-43be-b82a-a05422fd54d4)

However, this is not a complete list of names, logos, and brand features, all of which are subject to these guidelines.

If you want to report misuse of a OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark, please contact us via https://uav4geo.com/contact

## When do I need specific permission to use a OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark?

You may do the following without receiving specific permission from UAV4GEO:

 * Use OpenDroneMap WebUI wordmarks in text to truthfully refer to and/or link to unmodified OpenDroneMap WebUI programs, products, services and technologies.
 * Use OpenDroneMap WebUI logos in visuals to truthfully refer to and/or to link to the applicable programs, products, services and technologies hosted on UAV4GEO servers.
 * Use OpenDroneMap WebUI wordmarks to explain that your software is based on OpenDroneMap WebUI's open source code, or is compatible with OpenDroneMap WebUI's software.
 * Describe a social media account, page, or community in accordance with the [Social Media Guidelines](#social-media-guidelines).

All other uses of a OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark require our prior written permission. This includes any use of a OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark in a domain name. Contact us at https://uav4geo.com/contact for more information.

## When allowed, how should I use a OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark?

### General Guidelines

#### Do:

 * Use the OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark exactly as shown in the list above.
 * Use OpenDroneMap WebUI wordmarks only as an adjective, never as a noun or verb. Do not use them in plural or possessive forms. Instead, use the generic term for the OpenDroneMap WebUI product or service following the trademark, for example: OpenDroneMap WebUI processing software.

#### Don't:

 * Don't use OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks in the name of your business, product, service, app, domain name, publication, or other offering.
 * Don't use marks, logos, company names, slogans, domain names, or designs that are confusingly similar to OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks.
 * Don't use OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks in a way that incorrectly implies affiliation with, or sponsorship, endorsement, or approval by UAV4GEO of your products or services.
 * Don't display OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks more prominently than your product, service, or company name.
 * Don't use OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks on merchandise for sale (e.g., selling t-shirts, mugs, etc.)
 * Don't use OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks for any other form of commercial use (e.g. offering technical support services), unless such use is limited to a truthful and descriptive reference (e.g. Independent technical support for OpenDroneMap WebUI's software).
 * Don't modify OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks, abbreviate them, or combine them with any other symbols, words, or images, or incorporate them into a tagline or slogan.

 ### Social Media Guidelines

In addition to the General Guidelines above, the name and handle of your social media account and any and all pages cannot begin with an OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark. In addition, OpenDroneMap WebUI logos cannot be used in a way that might suggest affiliation with OpenDroneMap WebUI, including, but not limited to, the account, profile, or header images. The only exception to these requirements is if you've received prior permission from OpenDroneMap WebUI.

For example, you cannot name your account, page, or community "OpenDroneMap WebUI Representatives" or "OpenDroneMap WebUI Software". However, it would be acceptable to name your account, page, or community "Fans of OpenDroneMap WebUI" or "Information about OpenDroneMap WebUI Software" as long as you do not use the OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks or OpenDroneMap WebUI logos or otherwise suggest any affiliation with OpenDroneMap WebUI.

### Open Source Project Guidelines

OpenDroneMap WebUI's license and code says what you can and cannot do with the code itself but does not give permission to use OpenDroneMap WebUI's trademarks. If you choose to build on or modify OpenDroneMap WebUI's open source code for your own project,

#### You Must:

 * Follow the terms of the Open Source License(s) for OpenDroneMap WebUI software products and code.
 * Choose branding, logos, and trademarks that denotes your own unique identity so as to clearly signal to users that there is no affiliation with or endorsement by OpenDroneMap WebUI.
 * Follow the General Guidelines, above.

#### You Must NOT:

* Use any OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark in connection with the user-facing name or branding of your project.
 * Use any OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark or any part of any OpenDroneMap WebUI trademark to incorrectly suggest or give the impression your software is actually published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by OpenDroneMap WebUI.

For example, please do not name your project, [Something]-OpenDroneMap WebUI

#### You May:

 * State in words (not using logos or images) that your product "works with" or "is compatible" with OpenDroneMap WebUI, if that is true.
 * State in words (not using logos or images) that your project is based on OpenDroneMap WebUI open source technology, if that is true, as long as you also include a statement that your project is not officially associated with OpenDroneMap WebUI or its products.

For instance, you may state that your project:

"is proudly built from OpenDroneMap WebUI's open source software"

as long as you also include the statement equally prominently:

"[Brand Name] and [Product Name] are not officially associated with OpenDroneMap WebUI or its products."

### Cloud-based Services

If you offer a cloud-based service that provides remote access to OpenDroneMap WebUI software, it is important that you do so in a way that does not confuse users about who is offering the service. You must not use OpenDroneMap WebUI trademarks in the name of your product or service.

You may state in words (not using logos or images) that your product or service features or provides access to unaltered OpenDroneMap WebUI software, if this is true.

For example:

Acceptable: [Your Product Name] featuring OpenDroneMap WebUI's processing software

Incorrect: OpenDroneMap WebUI [Your Product Name]

### Community Guidelines

Various permissions to use OpenDroneMap WebUI Trademarks have been provided to various members of the community, and these OpenDroneMap WebUI Trademark Guidelines do not alter any such previously granted permissions.
